Dedue 的默认体格锁：

```text
Dedue must be very tall, broad-shouldered, thick-chested and powerfully built, a calm shield-wall guardian beside Dimitri, not a slim student.
```

负面约束：

```text
No skinny Dedue, no narrow shoulders, no average schoolboy body, no ordinary slim soldier build.
```
