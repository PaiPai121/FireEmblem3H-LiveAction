# EP02 Scene09 Existing Start/Key Substantive Audit

Date: 2026-07-22

Scope: the 36 existing raster start/key pairs in `episodes/EP02/generated/mock_battle_production_keyframes_2026-07-17/`, reviewed against `episodes/EP02/scene09_shot_list_v2_2026-07-21.md`.

## Decision

`blocked_needs_revision`

The existing frames are better than pure missing-material state: they establish a coherent outdoor mock-battle field and several readable tactical beats. But they are not reasonably approved as V2 production frames. The existing `qa_passed` labels mostly prove local delivery integrity and rough start/key association; they do not prove shot purpose, weapon locks, character identity, V2 semantic match, or video readiness.

## Blocking Issues

1. Several existing frames contradict the V2 shot purpose even though they have files and look polished.
2. Multiple start frames already show the key action, which breaks start-to-key motion logic.
3. Some shots are attractive group tableaux instead of tactical action peaks.
4. Several named-character beats are unreadable at production distance or replaced by generic formation/crowd logic.
5. Weapon/action locks remain unreliable: Mercedes glows too early, Felix/Ferdinand route block is not a wooden-sword block, and some lances read as short blades or generic poles.
