# Nian Beauty And Realism Direction

Nian should remain a cute, pretty, youthful young woman. Realism should remove AI/cosplay artifacts without reducing her charm.

## Desired Read

- young, beautiful, lively, mischievous;
- bright intelligent eyes, like she is already thinking of the next prank or note;
- teasing half-smile, not a blank idol smile;
- pretty enough to be character-forward, but photographed like a real person on a real set;
- modern young film director energy: playful, fast, capable, slightly chaotic.

## Real Human Beauty Reference Principles

Do not copy any real actor or celebrity likeness. Use real human photography as a quality bar for:

- natural cheek structure;
- slight facial asymmetry;
- realistic eye size and restrained catchlights;
- visible but delicate skin texture;
- natural lips and uneven smile;
- believable hair strands and flyaways;
- soft set-light interaction on skin and cloth.

## Avoid

- AI doll face;
- influencer filter face;
- hyper-smooth airbrushed skin;
- oversized anime eyes;
- perfect symmetrical face;
- glossy plastic cosplay wig;
- making her gritty, old, plain, harsh, tired, masculine, or unattractive.
