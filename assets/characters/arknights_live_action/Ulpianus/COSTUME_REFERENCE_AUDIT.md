- Pass/fail: white/silver hair and Abyssal Hunter cues
- Pass/fail: dark heavy hunter coat with sea-worn details
- Pass/fail: massive anchor/harpoon weapon silhouette
- Pass/fail: immovable commander stance
- Pass/fail: salt-worn leather and metal
- Pass/fail: face is distinct from all existing selected operators.
- Pass/fail: skin/hair/fabric read as real, not wax/plastic.
- Pass/fail: hands, visible props, and costume structure are not visibly broken.

## Blockers
