Eyes: sharp red-brown eyes, focused and vigilant; avoid dreamy or idol softness.

Nose: clean natural bridge, slightly stronger than Nian/Exusiai; avoid sameface delicate button nose.

Mouth/smile: restrained neutral mouth or slight stern set; no bright grin.

Skin/makeup: realistic human texture; makeup intensity character-specific.

Expression habit: disciplined, ready to issue an order or draw the sword.

Posture/camera relationship: upright command posture, one hand near sword or hip, tactical readiness.

Must not resemble: Nian, Wiš'adel, Texas the Omertosa, Exusiai, Surtr, or any future selected live-action operator face.

## Must Preserve

- Signature hair/silhouette: dark blue-purple long hair with side ponytail movement; black-orange horn/dragon ornaments; slim dragon tail if visible.
- Signature accessories: ID badge/lanyard, black gloves, sword and sheath, tactical harness.
- Outfit language: black open jacket with blue-white sleeve bands, white shirt and tie, black shiny shorts, high black boots, red-bladed sword. Do not turn into a generic police uniform.
- Color palette: black, white, blue-purple hair, red/orange sword and small accents.
- Role tools/props: sword and scabbard are identity-critical; set props must not hide them.
- Personality read: disciplined Lungmen officer energy; heroic intensity, not playful.

## Live-Action Translation

- What becomes realistic: cloth, skin, hair, practical props, lighting.
- What remains stylized: signature silhouette, palette, accessories, and character-specific expression.
- What must not become generic: face, hair, costume silhouette, and personality.
