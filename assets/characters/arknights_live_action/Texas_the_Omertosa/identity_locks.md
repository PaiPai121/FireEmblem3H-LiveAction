## Face Lock

Age/read: young adult; cool and lethal, not cute or bubbly.

Beauty type: quiet, cool, exhausted-but-deadly elegance

Face shape: long-soft oval with tired elegance; must differ from Surtr's sharper heat and Nian's mischievous cuteness.

Eyes: amber/orange, calm and slightly exhausted; direct, not wide-eyed.

Nose: subtle straight bridge, understated and natural.

Mouth/smile: nearly expressionless or faintly weary; no idol smile.

Skin/makeup: realistic human texture; makeup intensity character-specific.

Expression habit: quiet, controlled, inward, dangerous without showing off.

Posture/camera relationship: relaxed stillness, one hand near weapon, does not pose for glamour.

Must not resemble: Nian, Wiš'adel, Ch'en, Exusiai, Surtr, or any future selected live-action operator face.

AI risks: sameface, waxy skin, plastic wig, cosplay fabric, influencer filter face.

## Must Preserve

- Signature hair/silhouette: long black/dark charcoal hair, wolf ears, pale tail if visible, slim vertical silhouette.
- Signature accessories: blue glove/hand accent, white sleeve/glove, belt pouches, long weapon/sword silhouette.
- Outfit language: black fitted core over white blouse/sleeves, blue lower shorts/panel, dark thigh-high legwear, long dark coat/cape with pale lining, white boots. Do not turn into generic black leather assassin.
- Color palette: black, white, gray, cobalt blue accents, amber eyes.
- Role tools/props: long sword/weapon and tail are identity locks; do not hide both.
- Personality read: quiet Omertosa restraint; tired but deadly.

## Live-Action Translation

- What becomes realistic: cloth, skin, hair, practical props, lighting.
- What remains stylized: signature silhouette, palette, accessories, and character-specific expression.
- What must not become generic: face, hair, costume silhouette, and personality.
