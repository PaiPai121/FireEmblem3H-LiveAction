# Arknights Live-Action Casting Style Bible

Status: `active_global_rule`

Companion realism authority: `REALISM_STYLE_BIBLE.md`

## Age And Beauty Direction

All Arknights live-action operator assets in this directory use a young-adult casting target:

- Apparent age: about 18-22.
- Must read as clearly adult, never childlike.
- Face direction: young, beautiful, photoreal, natural skin texture, distinct facial structure per operator.
- Do not add visible aging, wrinkles, tired aging lines, elderly proportions, or middle-aged casting unless the user explicitly overrides this file.
- Beauty must stay compatible with real actor casting. A flawless AI-doll face, porcelain skin, glamour poster face, or repeated sameface template fails even when the character looks young and attractive.

## Female Operator Strict Addendum

Female operators must not be made older to communicate competence, command, combat experience, medical authority, judicial pressure, or emotional damage.

For selected female assets:

- Apparent age should land around 18-22, with a preference for 18-21 when the first pass reads too mature.
- The face must read young and pretty in a natural actor way: clear features, fresh face, believable skin, and distinct beauty.
- Severe, calm, cold, tired, veteran, judge, doctor, captain, or executive traits must be expressed through eyes, posture, costume, blocking, and props.
- Reject if makeup, facial structure, lighting, cheek hollows, under-eye treatment, mouth tension, or styling makes the performer read like a mature executive, middle-aged professional, older noble, or weary veteran.
- Reject if "realism" is achieved by making the face plain, harsh, tired, or older. Realism means photographed actor texture and practical wardrobe, not reduced beauty.

## Authority Without Aging

Some operators are doctors, generals, nobles, judges, veterans, captains, or strategists. Their authority must come from:

- costume accuracy;
- posture and blocking;
- eyes and expression;
- prop/weapon/device silhouette;
- grooming, lighting, and set context.

Authority must not come from making the actor look older.

## QA Rule

Reject or monitor any candidate that:

- looks older than early twenties;
- looks technically realistic but not young and pretty enough for the named female operator;
- turns a young-adult operator into a middle-aged or elderly performer;
- becomes a childlike idol, doll, mascot, or underage read;
- has a pretty face but loses the official outfit, color blocks, hair silhouette, weapon, or role identity;
- collapses into the same default AI face as an existing selected operator.
- looks like a polished AI/cosplay/fashion poster rather than a real film-production wardrobe fitting.

`selected.png` may only be created when the image is young-adult beautiful, recognizably the named operator, and passes the companion realism gate in `REALISM_STYLE_BIBLE.md`.
