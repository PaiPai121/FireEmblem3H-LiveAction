# 青狮小剧场 01：雅妮特的食物歌

## Project

Fire Emblem: Three Houses Live-Action - Blue Lions Comedy Short 01

## Working Title

《雅妮特的食物歌 / アネットのごはんの歌》

## Format

约 60 秒真人电影感角色喜剧短片。

## Premise

学院期的雅妮特在加尔古·玛库温室里独自浇花，边干活边唱原作支援桥段里的食物歌，还不自觉配上轻快步法。菲力克斯路过并完整听见。雅妮特当场社死，菲力克斯却只认真评价她的步法不错，最后嘴硬地帮她继续浇花。

## Source Basis

- Inspired by Felix / Annette C support in Fire Emblem: Three Houses.
- Use an adapted English food-song performance with Japanese spoken dialogue, preserving the recognizable food-song support-scene function.
- Do not publish a full verbatim lyric transcript in public-facing package files. Store only timing and source-reference slots here.

## Tone

轻喜剧、温室日常、角色性格喜剧。笑点来自雅妮特努力过头的可爱、菲力克斯不擅长表达的冷脸温柔，而不是恶搞或让角色崩坏。

## Characters

- Annette: academy phase, younger and cuter comedy-performance variant. Bright, earnest, easily flustered, sings when she thinks nobody is watching.
- Felix: academy phase. Sharp, blunt, quiet, accidentally sincere. His comedy comes from treating a silly song like combat footwork.
- Dedue: optional background or final quiet pass-by only if needed. Calm greenhouse competence; do not pull focus from Annette/Felix.

## Primary Location

Garreg Mach greenhouse interior corner: flower shelves, potted plants, watering can, wooden worktable, warm morning light.

## Asset Needs

- Younger/cuter Annette academy comedy-performance reference.
- Greenhouse interior corner reference, closer and more intimate than the existing exterior-wide greenhouse asset.

## Do Not Change

- Keep academy-era Blue Lions identity.
- Keep the scene rooted in the original support setup: Annette singing while watering plants; Felix overhears.
- Do not turn Felix into cruel mockery.
- Do not make Annette childish; she should read as a young academy student, not a child.
- No modern kitchen, no idol-stage performance, no musical fantasy effects.
