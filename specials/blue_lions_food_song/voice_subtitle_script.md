## Working Song

Use this complete adapted English food song unless production replaces it with an exact user-provided source line:

`Steaks and cakes, steaks and cakes, dinner's almost here. Savory meat and something sweet, dancing ever near.`

Canon meaning guide:

- Annette is singing a self-made food song while watering plants.
- The food images are deliberately silly: dinner, stewed meat, dessert, sweets, and meat arriving in a playful procession.
- The joke is not that she sings badly. The joke is that the song is private, hungry, earnest, and weirdly catchy.
- English localization is commonly remembered by "steaks and cakes"; this adapted English line keeps that player-recognition hook while the rest of the scene stays in Japanese dialogue.

Performance guide:

- Sing in tune, softly, like a student amusing herself while doing chores.
- Keep mouth movement small and private.
- No stage projection, no musical-theater belting, no idol performance.
- A tiny bounce in the rhythm is fine; big choreography is not.
