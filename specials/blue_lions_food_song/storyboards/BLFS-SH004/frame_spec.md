# BLFS-SH004 Frame Spec

## Shot Purpose

Turn the expected teasing moment into Felix's sincere footwork logic.

## Start Frame

Two-shot in the greenhouse aisle: Annette clutches the watering can, Felix stands one respectful step inside the doorway.

## Key Frame

Felix calmly says her footwork is good. Annette blinks, surprised because he is not teasing the song.

## End Frame

Annette reacts in flustered disbelief while Felix prepares to take over the watering.

## Identity Locks

Felix remains blunt, still, and composed. Annette remains flustered but not angry.

## Reject If

Reject if Felix laughs at her, if he becomes flirty, or if the shot loses the respectful distance between them.
