# BLFS-SH003 Frame Spec

## Shot Purpose

Land Annette's contained embarrassment and keep the comedy gentle.

## Start Frame

Close medium on Annette frozen with watering can lifted, eyes wide, shoulders rising.

## Key Frame

A tiny extra stream of water starts; Annette catches herself immediately before it becomes a spill.

## End Frame

Annette lowers the can and asks whether Felix also saw the little dance-like movements.

## Identity Locks

Annette is academy-age, bright, embarrassed, and earnest. No childish panic, no cartoon face, no slapstick soaking.

## Reject If

Reject if the gag humiliates Annette, if water explodes everywhere, or if her face drifts from the selected/reference identity.
