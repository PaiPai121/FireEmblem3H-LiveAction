# BLFS-SH002 Frame Spec

## Shot Purpose

Reveal Felix has overheard the song without making him mocking or intrusive.

## Start Frame

Annette is foreground finishing her turn. The greenhouse doorway is soft in the background.

## Key Frame

Focus lands on academy Felix standing quietly at the doorway. He watches Annette's foot placement and balance, not her face with mockery.

## End Frame

Annette notices Felix and freezes.

## Identity Locks

Felix: academy phase, dark blue hair, lean duelist posture, blunt quiet expression, no grin. Annette remains youthful and flusterable.

## Reject If

Reject if Felix smiles broadly, looks cruel, appears suddenly from nowhere, or if Annette does not clearly notice him.
