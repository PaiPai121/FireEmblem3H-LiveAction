# Director Treatment

## Intent

Make a tiny character comedy that feels discovered, not staged. The camera catches Annette in a private rhythm, then lets Felix's silence become the punchline.

## Visual Language

- Natural morning light through greenhouse glass.
- Warm plant greens, wood, stone, brass watering can, blue academy accents.
- Close enough for expressions, wide enough to see footwork and watering rhythm.
- No musical effects, sparkles, floating food, or fantasy performance visuals.

## Performance Notes

### Annette

Start small and private. She is not performing for anyone. Her song is a self-motivation habit that turns into movement before she notices. When she sees Felix, her face should go through three quick beats: blank freeze, realization, panic.

### Felix

Stillness is the joke. He does not smirk. He watches like a swordsman evaluating balance. When he says the footwork is good, it is sincere, not teasing.

### Relationship Tone

Keep it lightly sweet but not romantic-forward. This is a support-conversation seed: embarrassment, bluntness, tiny kindness.

## Camera Rules

- Use gentle handheld or slow locked moves.
- Do not orbit.
- Do not cut too fast during the song; the audience must see Annette's step-turn-pour rhythm.
- Felix reveal should be simple: either a rack focus or Annette turning into his eyeline.

## Sound

- Greenhouse ambience: water, glasshouse creak, distant monastery birds.
- Annette's song begins small and grows only a little.
- Felix's line should cut through the silence after the song.
- End with Felix's deadpan plant-singing warning, then let Annette's embarrassed protest breathe.

## Continuity Rules

- Watering can stays consistent.
- Annette's route: right-side flowers to center aisle, then left-side row remains dry for Felix's final action.
- Felix remains at the doorway until he steps in to take the watering can.

## Motion Risks

- AI video may over-musicalize the singing.
- Annette may age up if prompt says "beautiful woman"; use "youthful academy student" and "earnest, bright, flustered".
- Felix may become expressive or mocking; lock "blunt, quiet, no grin".
- Water physics may escalate; keep small pours only.
