# Dialogue And Audio Beats

Use as the audio/dialogue layer for the behind-the-scenes special. Dialogue should feel like captured set audio, not scripted stand-up. Keep lines short, natural, and driven by Nian and the four actors. Crew may be visible in frame but should not carry explanatory dialogue.

## Global Audio Tone

- Light production ambience: footsteps on dirt, fabric, wooden props, folding chairs, plates, quiet crew movement.
- Laughter is restrained and warm, never canned.
- No music unless added later in edit.
- No explicit AI/generation language.
- No monitor malfunction language.
- No staff exposition lines explaining the gag.

## BTS-SH000

Audio:

- The shot begins with serious mock-battle room tone, footwork, fabric, wooden training weapons and a faint boom/crew presence.
- Nian director, offscreen then visible, sharp but amused: "卡卡卡，拍的什么玩意？"
- Everyone stops.
- Nian: "停一下。Claude，你刚才撤到一半看这边了；Dimitri，脚过胶带；Edelgard，披风挂栏杆。还有那根杆子，别抬到灯底下，太亮。"
- Dimitri: "我是不是过线了？"
- Nian: "过了，半只脚。"
- Edelgard: "披风刚才卡了一下。"
- Nian: "看到了。下一条离栏杆半步，别让它替你停。"
- Claude: "我以为后面有人。"
- Nian: "可以，但这条先别管我这边。撤的时候看路。"
- Byleth: "再来一条。"
- Nian: "好，各回原位，再来。"

Laugh cue:

- One restrained cast/crew laugh after Nian's first diagnosis; no broad comedy.

## Reject If

- Dialogue becomes stand-up comedy or explains the joke after the image already shows it.
- Any character says or implies AI/generation/model/monitor failure.
- Crew members become exposition devices.
- Byleth becomes talkative.
- Nian's line feels genuinely abusive instead of directorial and amused.
