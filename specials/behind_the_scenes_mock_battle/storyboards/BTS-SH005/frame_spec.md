# BTS-SH005 Frame Spec

## Shot Purpose

Turn the NG into shared warmth through monitor playback and Byleth's quiet approval.

## Start Frame

Over-shoulder view near a director monitor station. Byleth, Edelgard, Dimitri, and Claude gather around the monitor in three-quarter profile. Crew silhouettes and cables are present but subdued.

## Key Frame

Claude points lightly at the playback, Dimitri bows his head in sincere apology, Edelgard almost smiles while trying not to, and Byleth watches the monitor.

## End Frame

Byleth gives one small approving nod. The others accept that the NG belongs in the extras. The group mood becomes relaxed and warm.

## Identity Locks

Byleth calm and minimal, Claude relaxed, Dimitri earnest, Edelgard restrained. Monitor content should be indistinct or simple, not a detailed hallucinated second scene.

## Reject If

Reject if Byleth becomes expressive or chatty, if the monitor shows unrelated footage, if faces crowd into unreadability, or if the group becomes generic crew.
