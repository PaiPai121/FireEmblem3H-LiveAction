# BTS-SH002 Frame Spec

## Shot Purpose

Show lunch-break character contrast through small behavior.

## Start Frame

Long dining table in Garreg Mach dining hall or crew lunch setup. Edelgard, Dimitri, Claude, and Byleth are seated in academy/professor costumes with plates and cups. The table contains food, napkins, and simple production labels, but no modern cafeteria branding.

## Key Frame

Claude is caught by the camera subtly sliding unwanted greens away. Dimitri's oversized training meal sits beside him, Edelgard's plate is arranged with almost ceremonial neatness, and Byleth remains quiet and centered. The joke must read through behavior, not mugging.

## End Frame

Camera lands on Byleth calmly eating while the others react around them: Claude gives a tiny guilty smile, Dimitri looks genuinely puzzled, Edelgard hides a restrained reaction.

## Identity Locks

Edelgard composed and exacting, Dimitri earnest, Claude playful but not modern, Byleth quiet and unreadable. Keep academy/professor costumes clean and consistent.

## Reject If

Reject if the scene becomes modern variety-show comedy, if food/hand anatomy drifts, if Byleth becomes chatty, or if any lead loses recognizable identity.
