# BTS-SH006 Frame Spec

## Shot Purpose

End with a warm wrap-photo image that feels like official extra material.

## Start Frame

Group arranges on monastery steps or training-ground group-photo setup. Main four are in front or centered; student extras and crew stand around them. A light stand or crew member is still stepping out.

## Key Frame

A clapperboard accidentally enters the edge of frame while Claude notices and plays to camera. Dimitri relaxes, Edelgard tries not to laugh, and Byleth remains quietly centered.

## End Frame

Everyone settles into a warm group photo. Byleth gives the faintest smile. The frame should hold like a final freeze-frame before fade.

## Identity Locks

Main four must remain readable. Extras can be generic academy students and crew, but they must not crowd or obscure the leads.

## Reject If

Reject if main four are unreadable, if the group becomes a random crowd, if modern gear dominates the image, or if the ending loses warmth.
