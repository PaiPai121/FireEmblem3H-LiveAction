# BTS-SH004 Frame Spec

## Shot Purpose

Create the main NG beat while preserving character dignity.

## Start Frame

Locked wide rehearsal frame on a mock battle lane with wooden fences. Byleth stands near a director monitor edge, Edelgard near a fence mark, Dimitri ready at his start mark, Claude centered with prop bow lowered. The assistant director is off to one side.

## Key Frame

The NG happens: Dimitri has overshot his mark by one clear step while remaining safe, Edelgard's cape catches lightly on the fence, and Claude turns the mishap into an improvised line. Byleth watches with quiet control.

## End Frame

Director calls cut. The cast relaxes; Edelgard frees the cape, Dimitri turns back apologetically, Claude smiles, and Byleth gestures for reset without speaking.

## Identity Locks

No injury, no slapstick fall, no lethal combat. Keep the mock battle nonlethal and respectful.

## Reject If

Reject if anyone falls, gets hurt, fires a weapon, becomes humiliated, or if the shot turns into chaotic combat instead of a controlled rehearsal NG.
