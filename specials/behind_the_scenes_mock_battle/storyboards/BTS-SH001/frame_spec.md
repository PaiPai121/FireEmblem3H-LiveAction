# BTS-SH001 Frame Spec

## Shot Purpose

Reveal that the serious mock battle is actually a film set behind the scenes.

## Start Frame

Close foreground clapperboard reading "Mock Battle - Behind the Scenes", held in front of a Garreg Mach training-ground backlot. Behind the slate, Byleth, Edelgard, Dimitri, and Claude are frozen in serious rehearsal marks. Wooden fences, soft training weapons, light stands, and a small crew presence are visible but controlled.

## Key Frame

The slate claps. The four actors begin relaxing out of their serious poses: shoulders lowering, weapons dropping to safe rest positions, crew moving in the background. The image must clearly read as a film set.

## End Frame

Camera has pulled back into a wider view: actors and crew share the same frame, light stands and sound equipment are visible, soft weapons are being collected, and the training-ground lane leads toward the next lunch-break beat.

## Identity Locks

Byleth professor look, Edelgard academy look, Dimitri academy look, Claude academy look. No war-era costumes. No lethal weapons.

## Reject If

Reject if the shot reads as an in-world battle, if the slate is missing, if crew/equipment vanish, if the main four become generic extras, or if weapons look lethal.
