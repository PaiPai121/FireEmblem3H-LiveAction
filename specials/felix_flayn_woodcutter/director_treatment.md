# Director Treatment

## Style

Live-action academy support comedy. Start serious, then reveal the mundane task. Keep the humor restrained and character-driven.

## Staging

Use one training-ground area with three visual zones:

- Felix practice lane.
- Flayn observation position near a woodpile.
- A cutting spot where logs are placed.

## Camera

Mostly locked medium shots and two-shots. Avoid flashy action coverage. The cutting gag should be clean and readable, not a full combat sequence.

## Performance

Felix:
- Minimal expression changes.
- Pride registers as tiny pauses and sharper posture.
- The final satisfaction should be almost denied.

Flayn:
- Hands clasped or lightly applauding.
- Praise is genuine.
- The wood request should feel polite enough that Felix cannot attack it directly.

## Video Model Risks

- Sword motion may drift into combat. Keep logs as the target and no enemies visible.
- Flayn may read as scheming. Keep her sincere.
- Felix may smile too broadly. Keep satisfaction subtle.
- Avoid readable text, subtitles, or woodpile labels.
