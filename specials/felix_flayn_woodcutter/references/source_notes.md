## Adaptation Boundary

This short adapts the Felix and Flayn C-support premise: Flayn watches Felix train, praises his sword technique, then reveals she wants help cutting firewood. Felix resists the errand framing but accepts the challenge when Flayn praises his ability. The scene ends with Flayn delighted by the result and Felix pretending not to care.

Dialogue is Japanese adapted text for the live-action short, not a verbatim reproduction of the support conversation.
