# Dialogue / Audio Beats

Language: Japanese
Subtitles: none
Music: none
Ambience: training ground air, distant wooden practice strikes, light cloth movement

| Time | Audio Beat |
| --- | --- |
| 0-12s | Felix trains; Flayn praises his sword technique. |
| 12-24s | Felix tells her this is not a show; Flayn pivots to asking about cutting something practical. |
| 24-36s | Felix sees the woodpile and refuses to be a firewood worker. |
| 36-48s | Flayn frames it as maybe too difficult; Felix accepts the challenge. |
| 48-60s | Felix cuts the pile; Flayn names him the monastery's finest woodcutter; Felix objects while secretly pleased. |

## Performance Notes

- Felix should be dry and clipped, never broadly comedic.
- Flayn should sound sincerely delighted, not manipulative or smug.
- The laugh comes from Felix's pride doing the work for her.
