# Outline

Runtime: 60 seconds
Location: Garreg Mach training ground, near weapon racks and a small woodpile.

## Beat Structure

1. Felix practices with precise sword forms.
2. Flayn watches and applauds with sincere admiration.
3. Flayn asks whether such sword skill can cut "something practical."
4. Felix realizes she means firewood and refuses the errand framing.
5. Flayn praises him into treating it as a challenge.
6. Felix cuts the whole pile with absurd discipline.
7. Flayn declares him the monastery's finest woodcutter.
8. Felix says not to call him that, while clearly enjoying the recognition.

## Visual Payoff

The scene starts like a serious martial-arts training moment and ends with Felix standing beside a perfectly split woodpile, trying to keep his dignity.
