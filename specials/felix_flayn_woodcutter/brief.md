## Premise

Felix is training with absolute seriousness. Flayn watches in awe, praises his swordsmanship, and slowly reveals she needs him to cut a pile of firewood. Felix says he is not a handyman, but Flayn's sincere confidence turns it into a challenge. He cuts everything too perfectly, then tries to act like he was never pleased by the praise.

## Why This Works

- One location and two characters.
- Clear visual action: sword practice becomes wood chopping.
- Strong personality contrast: Felix's pride vs Flayn's disarming sincerity.
- Support-scene fidelity without needing exact game text.

## Character Locks

Felix:
- Academy version, black hair, Blue Lions black uniform, sword at hip or in hand.
- Serious, terse, easily provoked by skill-based praise.
- Never goofy, never cruel.

Flayn:
- Academy/church version, mint-green hair, black robe.
- Warm, sincere, slightly mysterious, not mocking.
- Her politeness should make the request funnier.
