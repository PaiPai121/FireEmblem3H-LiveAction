# Visual Audit - Nian C-Support V2

Status: approved_for_video
Date: 2026-07-27

## Scope

Reviewed all 15 storyboard frames for the five-shot Sylvain x Ingrid C-support recreation with Nian as a SH001-only cameo.

## Findings

- SH001: Nian is present as the cameo trigger, Sylvain reads charming and overconfident, and the end frame supports Nian exiting before Ingrid enters.
- SH002: Nian is absent. Ingrid reads youthful and stern rather than jealous; Sylvain shifts into visible damage control.
- SH003: The close two-shot supports the old-friend accountability beat and grandmother-incident escalation without turning cruel or romantic.
- SH004: Ingrid's notes and Sylvain's shrinking posture support the scarecrow and noble-apology examples. No extra character takes focus.
- SH005: The scene resolves as friendship comedy: Ingrid is firm, Sylvain is sheepish, and the final beat avoids romance framing.

## Watch Items For Video Generation

- Preserve Ingrid's youthful face and school-age styling across lip-sync.
- Keep Nian out of SH002-SH005.
- Avoid subtitles, readable notes, or generated text on papers.
- Keep Sylvain embarrassed and playful, not angry or devastated.

Decision: approved_for_video.
