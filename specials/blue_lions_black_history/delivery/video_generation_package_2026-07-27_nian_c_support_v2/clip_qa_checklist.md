# Clip QA Checklist

Use this after generating each clip.

## Per-Clip Checks

- Clip duration matches `metadata.json`.
- First frame resembles `start_frame.png`.
- Middle beat resembles `key_frame.png`.
- Final frame resembles `end_frame.png`.
- Dialogue matches the `Dialogue / Vocal Lines` section in `prompt.md`.
- No subtitles, captions, logos, watermarks, or readable generated text.
- Faces stay close to the frame references during speaking.
- Camera does not spin, jump, or reverse screen direction.

## Story Checks

- SH001: Nian appears only as the cameo trigger and exits before SH002.
- SH002: Ingrid catches Sylvain after Nian has left.
- SH003: Ingrid invokes old apology history and the grandmother incident.
- SH004: Scarecrow and noble-apology examples land as public-pressure comedy.
- SH005: Ingrid tells Sylvain to change now; Sylvain's recovery smile fails lightly.

## Reject Immediately If

- Nian appears in SH002-SH005.
- Ingrid looks like a mature adult or reads as jealous.
- Sylvain becomes predatory, angry, or devastated.
- The ending becomes romantic.
- Any subtitle or visible text is added.
