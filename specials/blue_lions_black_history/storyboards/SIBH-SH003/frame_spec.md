# SIBH-SH003 Frame Spec

## Shot Purpose

Establish childhood familiarity and begin the C-support black-history list.

## Start Frame

Sylvain appeals to Ingrid as his important childhood friend; Ingrid remains unimpressed.

## Key Frame

Ingrid asks how many times she has had to apologize after him.

## End Frame

Ingrid lands the grandmother incident; Sylvain starts to worry about people hearing.

## Identity Locks

Ingrid remains youthful, calm, and precise. Sylvain remains embarrassed and playful rather than angry.

## Reject If

Reject if Ingrid becomes cruel, if Sylvain becomes hostile, if Nian reappears, or if close-up faces drift.
