# Production Notes

## Completion Target

Current target: prompt_asset_package.

This pass builds the script, shot list, storyboard frame prompts, video prompts, negative prompts, and manifests. Real start/key/end frame images are pending.

## Production Strategy

Keep the entire short in one monastery corridor or classroom doorway. Use two visible characters only. Implied students can be offscreen voices or background silhouettes if the video model handles them cleanly, but they are not required.

## Seedance-Oriented Constraints

- Five shots, total 60 seconds.
- Favor simple camera language and contained acting.
- Preserve academy-age faces and Blue Lions uniforms.
- Keep mouth motion small for dialogue.
- No subtitles, captions, visible written case files, UI text, logos, or watermarks.

## Comedy Rules

- Sylvain is embarrassed because Ingrid's examples are accurate.
- Ingrid is precise, not vindictive.
- The punchline should be a procedural correction: "This is not a lesson. It is a warning."
- The public pressure should be readable through Sylvain lowering his voice and glancing toward unseen students.

## Known Risks

- Sylvain may become too sleazy if the prompt overemphasizes flirting.
- Ingrid may read as jealous if the prompt overemphasizes romantic tension.
- Background students may multiply or steal focus.
- Text props may turn into unreadable generated glyphs.

## Blocking Items Before Video Generation Package

- Generate actual start/key/end frame images for all five shots.
- Run visual QA on generated frames.
- Run strict continuity after frames exist.
- Build immutable delivery package after all shots are approved_for_video.
