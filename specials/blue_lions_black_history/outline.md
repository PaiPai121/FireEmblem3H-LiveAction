# Outline

Title: Black History Audit
Runtime: 60 seconds
Location: Garreg Mach corridor / courtyard threshold

## Beat Structure

1. Sylvain spots Nian, a striking white-haired visitor, and approaches with an overly smooth compliment.
2. Nian gives him one amused, unreadable look and exits the scene.
3. Ingrid catches him and immediately recognizes the pattern.
4. Sylvain tries to frame it as harmless kindness and calls Ingrid an old friend.
5. Ingrid asks why an old friend is always the one apologizing after his behavior.
6. Ingrid names the childhood black-history examples: grandmother, scarecrow, and the noble apology.
7. Sylvain asks her to lower her voice because people can hear.
8. Ingrid refuses to let him dodge and tells him to start changing now.

## Scene Turn

Sylvain begins in effortless flirt mode and ends as the person Ingrid has been forced to manage for years.

## Visual Payoff

Nian's brief cameo creates the spark; Ingrid's calm memory turns the corridor into a public record of Sylvain's worst habits.
