# Dialogue / Audio Beats

Language: Japanese
Subtitles: none
Music: none
Ambience: Garreg Mach corridor, distant footsteps, faint student presence only when needed

## Timing

| Time | Audio Beat |
| --- | --- |
| 0-9s | Sylvain approaches Nian with a smooth compliment; Nian gives a small amused breath or silent look. |
| 9-21s | Ingrid cuts in; Sylvain claims it was harmless kindness. |
| 21-33s | Ingrid invokes their childhood-friend history and asks how often she has apologized for him. |
| 33-48s | Ingrid lists the scarecrow and noble-apology incidents; Sylvain asks her to lower her voice and accepts temporary defeat. |
| 48-60s | Ingrid tells him not to treat it as only today's loss; Sylvain quietly says he will try. |

## Performance Notes

- Sylvain starts warm and smooth, then gets quieter and faster.
- Ingrid stays even, crisp, and unhurried.
- Nian does not need spoken dialogue; her small amused reaction should not steal the scene.
- Silence after Ingrid's final correction should last just long enough for Sylvain's smile to fail.
