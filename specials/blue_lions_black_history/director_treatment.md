# Director Treatment

## Core Direction

Make a support-conversation comedy, not a classroom sketch. The first shot lets Sylvain make one confident mistake with Nian, then the camera hands the scene to Ingrid and stays there. Nian's slight cross-world presence is accepted visually but never explained in dialogue.

## Performance

### Sylvain

Begin smooth and effortless. The opening compliment should feel practiced but not predatory. Once Ingrid appears, let his charm become defense: smaller smiles, faster explanations, worried glances toward unseen listeners.

### Ingrid

Stay calm, precise, and youthful. She is not jealous. She is the old friend who has had to repair the damage too many times. Her comedy comes from refusing to overreact while saying increasingly embarrassing facts.

### Nian

One-shot cameo only. She is amused and self-possessed, as if Sylvain has accidentally walked into someone else's joke. She does not become a third lead.

## Camera

- SH001: medium/wide three-quarter framing to show Sylvain approaching Nian.
- SH002-SH003: two-shot confrontation, Ingrid left and Sylvain right.
- SH004: tighten slightly as old examples stack and Sylvain lowers his voice.
- SH005: hold a quiet two-shot for the final correction and Sylvain's failed recovery.

## Blocking Rules

- Nian exits or is absent after SH001.
- Ingrid enters from screen-left and keeps the authority position.
- Sylvain starts confident and gradually backs toward the wall/door.
- Offscreen students are implied by eyelines and faint sound, not visible crowd shots.

## Sound

Japanese dialogue only, no subtitles. Corridor ambience should be quiet enough for speech. A tiny offscreen murmur can occur after a black-history example, but no laugh track.

## Editing Rhythm

Start with the cameo hook, then cut cleanly into a support-style exchange. The main escalation is verbal: grandmother, scarecrow, apology. The final beat lands on Ingrid's correction, not on Nian.
