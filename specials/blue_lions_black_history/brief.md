# Blue Lions Black History

Project ID: blue_lions_black_history
Format: 60-second comedy short
Status: plan_package_candidate
Primary characters: Sylvain Jose Gautier, Ingrid Brandl Galatea
Cameo trigger: Nian
Era: Academy phase
Setting: Garreg Mach monastery corridor / courtyard threshold

## Premise

Sylvain tries to charm a strange white-haired visitor, Nian, as she passes through Garreg Mach. Nian only needs one amused look before leaving the scene. Ingrid catches Sylvain in the act and the short pivots into a C-support-inspired confrontation: childhood familiarity, public embarrassment, old flirting disasters, and Ingrid's long history of cleaning up after him.

## Source / Adaptation Basis

Use the Sylvain/Ingrid C support as the core event chain. The short should feel like a compact recreation of the support's famous black-history beat, not the previous social-skills lesson setup.

The dialogue is adapted Japanese dialogue, not game-original text. Preserve the support's recognizable incidents and function: Ingrid knows Sylvain too well, has cleaned up after him, and can dismantle his charm with calm specificity.

## Cameo Rule

Nian appears only in SH001 as the person Sylvain approaches. She has visual presence and a lightly amused reaction, but no major dialogue and no later story role. Her slight cross-world feeling is allowed; do not explain it.

## Tone

- Fast, dry, character-driven.
- Ingrid is not jealous or cruel; she is tired, precise, and protective of others.
- Sylvain begins charming and playful, then becomes smaller because Ingrid is too accurate.
- Nian is amused, not offended, and does not become the protagonist.
- The comedy is embarrassment, not humiliation.
- No romance-forward staging.

## Language

Japanese spoken dialogue. No subtitles in the video generation pass.

## Must Keep

- Sylvain is caught mid-flirt in SH001.
- Ingrid enters with the authority of someone who has known him since childhood.
- The C-support old-history incidents are represented: grandmother, scarecrow, and Ingrid apologizing on his behalf.
- Sylvain notices people can hear and asks Ingrid to lower her voice.
- Ingrid ends with a corrective line rather than a romantic beat.

## Reject If

- Nian remains after SH001 as a third lead.
- Ingrid reads as jealous, mean, or romantic possessive.
- Sylvain becomes predatory, creepy, or irredeemable.
- The scene becomes a modern phone/social-media sketch.
- Extra students become the focus.
- The camera mocks either character instead of letting their contrast create the joke.
