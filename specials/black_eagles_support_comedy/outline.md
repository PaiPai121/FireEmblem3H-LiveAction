# Four-Episode Outline

- EP01: Caspar x Linhardt B - Tall Opponent Tactics - Caspar cannot beat taller opponents, begs Linhardt for a strategy, learns a defensive counterplan, then drags Linhardt into exhausting practice until Linhardt escapes.
- EP02: Ferdinand x Dorothea C/B - Why Am I A Bee - Dorothea challenges Ferdinand to guess why she dislikes him and says he is like a bee; in the B support he presents self-made pastries as his earnest but wrong solution.
- EP03: Bernadetta x Caspar C - The Scenic View - Bernadetta panics when Caspar appears; Caspar concludes outside friendship is easy and carries her to a beautiful view, terrifying her before she admits the scenery is lovely.
- EP04: Petra x Bernadetta C - Like Prey - Bernadetta tries to ask Petra whether they can be friends; Petra sincerely says Bernadetta resembles prey, explains rabbit-like alertness, and accidentally terrifies her with bow safety talk.
