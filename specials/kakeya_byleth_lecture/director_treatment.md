## Directing Thesis

Treat the mathematics like tactical instruction. Female Byleth is not performing enthusiasm; she is testing whether students can see a hidden structure beneath a simple training object. The camera should make every mathematical turn feel like a change in battlefield rule.

## Performance

Female Byleth:
- Still, precise, almost surgical.
- Lets silence carry surprise.
- Uses short questions before explanations.
- Never smiles broadly; occasional faint softening when a student notices the right idea.

Students:
- 梅尔赛德斯: honest physical intuition; gently asks whether the sword needs a whole circle.
- 艾黛尔: rigorous constraint-checking; asks whether the construction is valid.
- 莉丝缇亚: pattern-seeking and precise; notices boundary and direction logic quickly.
- No off-roster students in this short; visible students are locked to one representative from each house.

## Visual Grammar

### Classroom

Use centered, stable frames when defining rules. Female Byleth controls the visual field: chalkboard, parchment, desks, windows. The lesson should feel disciplined.

### Training Ground

Use overhead and low oblique shots. The wooden sword, rope boundary, sand, and colored chalk must be readable. This is where abstract geometry becomes physical.

### Abstract Space

Use parchment-white or dark chalkboard negative space with clean colored geometry. Avoid full magical VFX except subtle Seiros-green glows tracing direction sets. The audience must read the diagram before admiring the style.

## Camera Progression

- Act 1: physical and grounded, with real sword and rope.
- Act 2: measured classroom geometry, chalk and parchment.
- Act 3: increasingly top-down and animated, showing cutting/sliding/overlap.
- Act 4: clean abstract tube diagrams, less character coverage.
- Act 5: return to Female Byleth and the sword, integrating story and concept.

## Editing Rhythm

Average shot duration: 7-18 seconds. Formula shots get longer holds. Perron tree sequence can accelerate through iterations with rhythmic cuts, but must pause at the first cut-slide-overlap step.

## Audio

- Sparse room tone, chalk, wood sword on stone, parchment scrape, rope pulled over sand.
- No bombastic music. Use a low string pulse only when Besicovitch reversal begins.
- Formula reveals get small sound design ticks, not magical explosions.

## Visual Motifs

- The sword is always red-marked or red-lit when representing the current unit segment.
- Preserved directions appear as green fan bands.
- Spent area appears as gold wash that shrinks during Perron iterations.
- Modern `delta`-tubes appear blue and translucent.

## Reject If

- Camera hides the geometry.
- Character close-ups interrupt the proof at the exact moment the audience needs diagrams.
- The Perron step happens too fast to see cut, slide, and overlap separately.
- The final meaning drifts into unrelated lifestyle analogies.
