# EP4 Project Bible — 女神再生仪式

## Episode identity

EP4 is a Blue Lions-centered live-action adaptation of the White Clouds chapter event commonly titled “女神再生仪式”. The episode moves from church ceremony and institutional calm into the buried Holy Tomb, then cuts to the red forest conspiracy scene before ending in a private Byleth–Sothis question. The adaptation preserves the source order and the contradiction at its center: the Church presents the rite as sacred continuity while the same rite exposes an armed, hidden conflict.

## Dramatic thesis

Sacred order is most frightening when it remains polite. The episode should feel like a sealed door opening by a few centimeters: first ceremony, then trespass, then a controlled encounter with the Death Knight, then an antagonist who speaks as if the rite belongs to nobody. The final emotional state is not “victory”; it is Byleth and Sothis realizing that every institution in the room has a second vocabulary.

## Canon and adaptation locks

- Preserve the event order visible in the supplied recording: opening illustration/rite context → monastery briefing → Holy Tomb mission → Death Knight threat → church aftermath/investigation → Flame Emperor forest exchange → Byleth and Sothis coda.
- Keep the Blue Lions as the audience’s human anchor. Dimitri is disciplined and protective, Dedue is spatially observant, and Byleth leads through short decisions rather than speeches.
- Rhea is serene, ceremonial, and institutionally absolute. Do not play her as a moustache-twirling villain.
- Death Knight is a readable, physically present threat. He is not a generic black knight and does not become a blood spectacle.
- Flame Emperor remains helmeted and strategically opaque. The scene is a question about ownership and the rite, not a full exposition dump.
- The game recording is a source/layout reference only. No game UI, tactical grid, dialogue box, damage number, watermark, or screenshot texture may appear in a final generated frame or clip.

## Performer and age locks

Every visible named performer is an adult performer portraying the academy-era character, perceived adult-safe 21–28 unless the role is canonically an adult faculty member. No child, teenager-coded face, war-phase aging, elderly drift, sexualized framing, or fetishized armor. Rhea, Seteth, Manuela, and Hanneman are adult faculty; Death Knight and Flame Emperor are fully helmeted identity roles and must remain physically coherent rather than becoming faceless CGI silhouettes.

## Language and audio locks

- Executable video prompts use Japanese spoken dialogue exactly once where a line is declared in the authoritative timing file.
- No Chinese/Mandarin voiceover, subtitles, captions, dialogue boxes, or readable text in-frame.
- Until a downstream native-audio probe passes, dialogue is a prompt/timing requirement, not an approved final audio delivery.
- Bell, wet stone, cloth, armor, breath, distant chanting, and controlled low-frequency impacts carry the atmosphere.
