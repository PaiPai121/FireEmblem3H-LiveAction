## Global Execution Rules

- Generate clips in shot order unless batching requires grouping by location.
- Preserve character identities from the referenced stills and selected asset notes.
- Keep all academy-phase students youthful; no war-phase aging or armor unless explicitly listed.
- No game UI, subtitles, readable text, watermarks, modern objects, gore, or lethal battlefield escalation.
- For training shots, keep all action controlled and nonlethal.
- After generation, review motion for identity drift, face swaps, costume drift, and continuity between adjacent shots.
