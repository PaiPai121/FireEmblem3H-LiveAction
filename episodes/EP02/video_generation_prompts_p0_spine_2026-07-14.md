## Global Video Rules

- Use each listed still as the first-frame or primary visual reference.
- Live-action cinematic fantasy drama, realistic fabric, stone, candlelight, and restrained acting.
- No game UI, no subtitles, no readable text, no watermark, no anime, no plastic cosplay.
- Keep academy-phase students young. Do not age Dimitri, Edelgard, Claude, Felix, Dedue, or Sylvain.
- Church authority should feel serene, formal, and politically heavy, not cartoon-villainous.
- The Flame Emperor must remain masked and unreadable. Do not reveal identity.
