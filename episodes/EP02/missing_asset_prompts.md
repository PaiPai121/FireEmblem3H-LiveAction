## LibraryOrTeacherOffice

目标路径建议：

`assets/locations/EP02/act01/LibraryOrTeacherOffice/LibraryOrTeacherOffice_reference.png`

Prompt:

Ultra-photorealistic live-action fantasy film interior concept image. A private teacher's office or small library inside Garreg Mach monastery at night, used for a quiet father-child warning scene between a veteran mercenary and the new professor. Medieval academy-monastery architecture: dark wood bookshelves, old books, parchment maps, a heavy desk, simple chairs, candlelight, stone walls, arched window, restrained church-academy decor, a few sealed letters and teaching materials. Warm low candlelight, deep shadows, intimate serious mood, not horror. The room should feel official but private, a place where someone can close the door and speak honestly. 35mm cinematic wide shot, grounded realistic materials, high-budget live-action fantasy drama, no people, no text, no readable labels, no modern furniture, no electric lamps, no UI, no watermark.

必须保留：

- 夜间室内、暖烛光、暗木书架。
- 重桌、旧书、地图、教案或信件。
- 私密谈话空间，不是宏伟礼堂。

避免：

- 现代办公室、现代台灯、电线、玻璃办公桌。
- 豪华宫殿、宴会厅、宝库感。
- 恐怖地牢、废墟、血腥。
