## 通用风格

Live-action cinematic fantasy drama, ultra photorealistic, high-budget medieval academy monastery, restrained performances, clean natural skin, realistic fabrics, grounded armor, sacred church authority, youthful academy tension, no game UI, no text, no watermark, no anime, no cartoon, no over-saturated color.

Negative prompt:

game UI, dialogue box, subtitle, text, watermark, logo, anime, cartoon, plastic cosplay, modern school uniform, modern buildings, guns, neon, science fiction, crowded market, comedy overacting, distorted face, bad hands, extra limbs.

## Sequence A - Arrival At Garreg Mach

Video prompt:

Wide live-action cinematic shot of a bright morning mountain road leading to a vast stone monastery built into the mountains. Byleth and Jeralt walk with a small escort of church knights. The road is wet stone and mud, with pale sunlight and thin mist. Jeralt looks wary while Byleth stays silent. The monastery feels sacred and powerful, not sinister. Slow tracking shot, restrained performance.

中文判断：山道要明亮，不要做成黑暗魔王城。Jeralt 的警惕是表演重点。

## Sequence B - Main Gate Plaza

Video prompt:

Live-action widescreen shot of the broad stone plaza before Garreg Mach's main gate. Ordered church guards stand in formation. A few students watch from colonnades and steps. Byleth and Jeralt enter through the center, feeling observed. Dimitri stands at the side and gives a polite formal nod. Bright clean daylight, academy-monastery authority, ceremonial stillness.

中文判断：重点是“新来者被全修道院审视”，不要热闹集市感。

## Sequence C - Cathedral Antechamber Audience

Video prompt:

Epic live-action cathedral antechamber. Golden-white holy light pours from high stained glass. Rhea stands in the light, serene and unreadable; Seteth stands slightly behind her, stern and cautious. Jeralt faces them with restrained discomfort; Byleth stands silent beside him. Strong axial composition, sacred authority, quiet pressure, realistic stone textures.

Dialogue beat:

Rhea welcomes Jeralt back, thanks Byleth, and offers the teaching post. Jeralt questions the offer. Rhea answers with gentle but immovable authority.

中文判断：Rhea 不能像反派，Seteth 不能像普通秘书；两人代表教会秩序。

## Sequence D - Three Houses Survey

Video prompt:

Live-action monastery courtyard montage introducing three student houses. Black Eagles appear composed and political, Golden Deer appear relaxed and watchful, Blue Lions appear courteous but tense. Focus on Dimitri greeting Byleth with formal sincerity, Dedue silent behind him, Felix skeptical, Sylvain deflecting with a smile, Mercedes calming the group, Annette nervous, Ashe earnest, Ingrid disciplined. Bright academy courtyard, stone arches, house banners, restrained ensemble acting.

中文判断：其他两学级只作对照；青狮是情感中心。

## Sequence E - Choosing Blue Lions

Video prompt:

Formal live-action audience hall. Rhea asks Byleth to choose a house to teach. The three house leaders stand separated by banners and light. Byleth looks from Edelgard to Claude to Dimitri, then quietly chooses Blue Lions. Dimitri's face shows surprise, gratitude, and responsibility. Seteth records the decision. Ceremonial, quiet, pivotal.

中文判断：这是章节转折，不要拍成菜单选择。

## Sequence F - Jeralt Warning

Video prompt:

Night interior in a monastery library or teacher's office. Jeralt closes the door and privately warns Byleth about Rhea and the church. Warm candlelight, dark wood shelves, old books, quiet father-child tension. Jeralt places an old mercenary badge on the table as a reminder of identity. Static camera, intimate dialogue, no melodrama.

中文判断：这场是父亲线核心。台词少，但每句都要有重量。

## Sequence G - Blue Lions Classroom

Video prompt:

Live-action Officers Academy classroom. Blue Lions students sit as a new class: Dimitri formal, Dedue quiet, Felix impatient, Sylvain relaxed, Mercedes warm, Annette anxious, Ashe attentive, Ingrid upright and disciplined. Byleth stands at the front, places a training sword on the desk, and says the first lesson will happen at the training ground. Natural daylight, stone academy interior.

中文判断：用坐姿和反应建立群像，不靠旁白介绍。

## Sequence H - Mock Battle

Video prompt:

High-budget live-action academy training ground mock battle. Students use blunted swords, wooden lances, training bows, and controlled magic under teacher supervision. Blue Lions execute a disciplined but imperfect formation. Ashe suppresses from high ground, Annette nearly loses confidence and Mercedes steadies her, Sylvain draws attention, Ingrid flanks, Felix breaks formation and is stopped by Byleth, Dedue protects Dimitri, Dimitri almost overcommits but restrains himself after Byleth's signal. Bright outdoor training field, banners, healers waiting, no blood, no lethal combat, no game grid UI.

中文判断：把游戏战斗改成可拍的训练演习；重点是 Byleth 获得学生信任，以及 Dimitri 暗线。

## Sequence I - Rhea Watches

Video prompt:

Long-lens live-action shot from the edge of the training ground. Rhea and Seteth watch Byleth teaching the Blue Lions. Rhea remains serene, Seteth remains doubtful. The students celebrate quietly in the distance, but the foreground is still and political. Sacred authority observing youth and war training.

中文判断：这场告诉观众“教会也在观察 Byleth”。

## Sequence J - Sothis Corridor

Video prompt:

Night monastery cloister. Byleth walks alone through candlelit stone arches. The world falls silent; faint green light ripples across stained glass. Sothis appears only as a brief reflection or inner vision, teasing and mysterious, warning that Byleth is entangled in strange events. Quiet supernatural moment, subtle, no magic explosion.

中文判断：Sothis 不要实体化太久，像意识里的突然插话。

## Sequence K - Flame Emperor Cliffhanger

Video prompt:

Dark mountain ruins at night, wet stone and low fog. Kostas, battered and angry, kneels before the Flame Emperor. The Flame Emperor wears a red horned mask, silver helmet sides, red feather-like shoulder fins, black-red cloak, and stands above him with calm menace. The scene reframes the bandit attack as a failed plan. Cinematic villain reveal, no gore, no game UI, no readable text.

中文判断：这是章末钩子。炎帝不能像普通红甲骑士，必须有“隐藏身份”和“计划者”的压迫感。
