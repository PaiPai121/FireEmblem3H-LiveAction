## Scene Contract

Purpose: Byleth wins the Blue Lions' first trust test by turning a three-house tactical mess into a narrow objective victory.

Conflict: Blue Lions must break through the center without exposing support. Black Eagles must hold discipline while Golden Deer exploit side gaps. Golden Deer must attack both sides enough to nearly steal the tempo. Teachers hold the northeast healing sigil as the objective.

Emotional turn: Blue Lions begin unsure whether Byleth can command students. By the end, they have evidence: Byleth reads the field, protects support, restrains reckless talent, and wins without humiliating anyone.

Reject if:

- Any house looks like disposable losers.
- Golden Deer only attack Blue Lions.
- Blue Lions win by knocking students down instead of moving teachers off the sigil.
- Dedue appears mortally wounded or Mercedes performs a saintly miracle pose.
- Named characters face camera during active combat while the threat/objective is elsewhere.
- Hilda, Annette, Ashe, Ignatz, Lorenz, Dorothea, or Ferdinand appear as generic adult mannequins.

## Beat 01 - Rules And Objective

Visible action: Two separated faculty objective markers sit on the northeast platform, not a shared combat post. Hanneman stands at the Black Eagles-side crest marker and checks wood weapons and padded armor; Manuela stands several paces away at the Golden Deer-side healing marker and raises one hand. Three houses wait at separate edges, not mixed together.

Facing: Students look toward the platform and their lanes, not the camera. Manuela faces southwest toward the Golden Deer-side route. Hanneman faces south/southeast down the Black Eagles-side steps. They are parallel faculty judges, not a paired fighting unit.

Dialogue:

**Manuela**: 规则很简单。木制武器，钝刃，禁止攻击无防备者。倒地、失去武器、或被教师判定压制，就退出场地。

**Manuela**: 两条教师裁定线就是目标。谁能让两名裁定教师都离开各自的标记，谁就取得本场胜利。

**Hanneman**: 我会负责裁定。若有人把模拟战当成真正的战场，我会亲自把他请下去。

Result: The audience understands this is a tactical objective match.

Visible action: Hilda, youthful academy appearance, training axe low, moves out from the west rail. Lorenz steps beside her to threaten Sylvain/Ingrid's lane. Ignatz marks a route with precise arrows, forcing Black Eagles side support to move. Sylvain turns serious; Ingrid's lance blocks Lorenz.

Visible action: Byleth checks the northeast platform, then keeps Dimitri/Dedue pressing instead of recalling them. Blue front remains north-facing. Side line bends west but does not break.

Facing: Byleth stands between central and side lanes, body turned toward northeast objective, hand cutting forward.

## Beat 12 - Three-Way Compression

Visible action: Blue Lions center keeps pressing north. Black Eagles adjust laterally to avoid Golden Deer arrows. Golden Deer lose space as both sides react. Byleth does not chase Golden Deer; he points Blue Lions pressure so Black Eagles move toward the already compressed west-side zone.

Facing: Everyone faces active threats and lanes: Blue north/northeast, Black south/southeast then west, Golden east/southeast. Byleth stands readable as the only person looking toward the objective window.

Dialogue:

**Byleth**: 不要追金鹿。把黑鹫往他们那边压。

**Byleth**: 前线不退。侧翼不断。把他们挤出去。

Result: The narrow Blue Lions window is earned by both rival houses being effective at the same time.

Manuela, several paces apart on the Golden Deer-side marker, lifts one hand to slow Sylvain's route; Ingrid breaks from the side lane to block Manuela's return path. ## Beat 14 - Objective Win And Restraint

Visible action: Ashe arrows pin Hanneman's spell geometry on the Black Eagles-side marker. Byleth enters from Hanneman's blind side and taps the casting wrist/forearm with a sheathed sword, forcing Hanneman off his own mark. Across the platform, Ingrid's lance blocks Manuela's return to her separate marker. Dimitri's lance stops near the central gap rather than lunging into either teacher; his hand is too tight. Byleth raises palm down.

Facing: Byleth faces Hanneman's casting side, not camera. Dimitri faces the split objective gap. Ingrid faces Manuela on the opposite marker. Mercedes remains behind line, healing light low and controlled.

Dialogue:

**Hanneman**: 判断得很好。

**Byleth**: 收。

**Dimitri**: 是。

Result: Blue Lions win the objective; Dimitri's darker tension is planted without breaking mock-battle safety.

Facing: Wide field composition preserves southeast Blue Lions, north/northeast Black Eagles, west Golden Deer, northeast teachers.

Dialogue:

Claude and Edelgard concession lines may bridge across this beat if needed.
