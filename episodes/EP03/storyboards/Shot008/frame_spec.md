## Frame Chain

- START: Catherine has just crossed the left arch threshold, seen from behind and three-quarter shoulder. Her white armor and mature posture interrupt the students' preparation. Thunderbrand is partly covered by a dark cloth wrap, but the jagged gold guard and purple crest stone are readable at her hip.
- KEY: Catherine crosses two steps through the group in a low tracking medium shot. She turns her head toward Byleth and Ashe while one gloved hand rests near the sheathed Thunderbrand. The cloth shifts with her walk; the weapon remains attached and unlit.
- END: Catherine stops just ahead of Byleth and Ashe and turns back toward them. Her hand remains near the guard, Ashe holds her eyeline, and Byleth stays visibly female in the middle distance. She delivers the Japanese line once, then the group is ready to turn toward the monastery gate.

Camera: Low tracking shot into a medium close shot; motivated movement, rain-wet reflections, no heroic weapon push-in.
Blocking: Left arch entrance -> cross the students -> stop and establish the authority/eyeline before the gate departure.
Action Peak: Catherine's controlled stop and the students' recognition of the weapon and her certainty, not a drawn-sword action.
Dialogue / Audio Anchor: Catherine, in Japanese: 霧の中では、私から離れすぎるな。 Add armor weight, cloth drag, wet footsteps, rain, and courtyard tone. No subtitles or UI.
